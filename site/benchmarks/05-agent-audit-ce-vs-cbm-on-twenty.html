Case Study 05: Autonomous agent audit on twentyhq/twenty (ce vs. CBM).

Two identical Gemini Flash agents were given the same 4-part architectural audit on the public twentyhq/twenty monorepo (46 packages, 24,527 tracked files, ~2.49M LOC): one equipped with CodeExplorer (v1.75.0) and one equipped with codebase-memory-mcp (v0.12.1). Both graphs were pre-indexed before the run.
